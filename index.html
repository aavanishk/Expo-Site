Top pick: Emitii

One workspace, shaped around how your team works.

What it is

Emitii, a new launch from Gurzu, puts Spaces, Board, Docs, Chat, Timesheets and AI in one place. Teams switch on only what they need: a Space per team (Marketing, Business Development, QA, Software Engineering), a board for tasks, docs beside the work and AI to help draft and update.

Why it stands out

  • A real product, not a service pitch: start free, demo QR on the flyer.
  • Backed by 12+ years of Gurzu delivery, 200+ products shipped, hubs in the US and Nepal.
  • Sells to any team, not only to banks or factories.
  • Room to grow: Nepali-language AI, local payments and invoicing, templates for schools, agencies and government offices.

The big picture

The expo shows a market that is mature in classic business software (ERP, HR, billing, SMS) and just starting to add AI, security and banking APIs on top. Most firms are Kathmandu-valley companies selling to Nepali banks, hotels, schools, government and SMEs, with local rules (IRD, Loksewa, SSF, Labour Act) as their main selling point.

What dominates

  • Business operations software: EOS, Trevo, Nimble, Basiyo, Talent Connects.
  • AI as a label: AakashTel, EKbana, Crimson Tech, Quickfox, ThirdFactor, Emitii.
  • Local compliance: IRD billing, SSF, Loksewa, connectIPS, eSewa, Khalti.

What is thin

  • Education, agriculture, logistics and health-records software.
  • Published prices, public case studies and proof of AI results.
  • Integration between vendors; each pitches its own “one platform”.

Note: Nimble’s older profile says 14 years; its newer flyer says 16+.

Business operations and ERP

EOS Web Solutions

Pepsicola, Kathmandu · eoswebsolutions.com

“Smarter management for every business.” It sells two products on one platform for running inventory, sales and billing, and the software is IRD certified.

  • EOS-RMS (restaurants): POS and billing, pay-first or pay-later flows, a cloud kitchen dashboard for online and delivery orders, loyalty and customer khata (credit tracking), table management and sales reports.
  • EOS-RETMS (retail): real-time inventory control, multi-store stock sync, barcode fast billing, SKU-level profit insights, product management and centralized sales.

Clients shown: G Café, GP Builders, Nutriblends, EZPlay, HGS (Hamro Gaming Store) and CIPL, with short testimonials about simpler sales and stock management.

Trevo ERP

Powered by Dots & Dashes · trevocloud.com

A flexible ERP that “adapts to your business”, aimed at companies with several branches.

  • Modules: finance (books, budgeting, assets), CRM, procurement (requests, RFQ, orders, GRN), HR and payroll, inventory (warehouses, batches, serial and IMEI tracking), sales, manufacturing (production planning, BOM, MRP, work orders, costing), POS and Trevo Retail, and mPOS for field and offline sales.
  • Platform: multi-company and multi-branch, highly customizable, open APIs, dynamic workflow and approvals, web, mobile and offline use, cloud or on-premise.
  • Nepal-specific: connectIPS for vendor and government payments, IRD eBilling and VAT reporting, credit and debit notes, and franchise or outlet e-commerce sync.

Basiyo Cloud PMS

Hotel software · cloud.basiyo.com

A hotel property management system pitched as “Run your hotel smarter, faster,” with up to 45% lower operating costs.

It covers reservations and front desk, housekeeping and maintenance, a channel manager with metasearch integration, restaurant management, stock, HR and staff management, ancillary services, multi-property management, and Nepal-compliant accounting and reporting.

It offers a trial booking and a QR code to its site.

Nimble Infosys

Tinkune, Kathmandu · since 2010 · nimble.com.np

A Nepali enterprise software house with 500+ organizations as clients, 500,000+ employee records managed and 70+ staff. Its mission is “to help society move forward through technology.”

  • Nimble HRMS: recruitment and onboarding, employee records and letters, payroll, tax, SSF and PF, loans and overtime, leave and GPS attendance, 360° appraisal, training, self-service app, travel and reimbursement, and exit settlement. It claims 40–50% lower operating cost and can post payroll to SAP, Finacle, MFin or Fact.
  • Nimble FIMS: fixed assets, stock and purchasing branch by branch, with QR labels, company and tax depreciation, vendor records, issue and transfer tracking, insurance and maintenance, and retirement, merge or split of assets. It covers 70% of banks and financial institutions, with 150+ organizations and 1,000,000+ assets.
  • DMS and Workflow: letter darta and chalani, memo and tippani approvals, and digital signature.
  • Online Recruitment: Loksewa-format applications, fee payment through eSewa, Khalti or IPS, admit cards and reports, with 50k+ candidates handled.
  • Delivery: SaaS, or a licence installed on the client’s own servers.

Talent Connects

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certified

“The HR technology and consulting company,” with three offerings.

  • HR software: an AI-powered ATS integrated with LinkedIn, employee self-service, records, attendance, leave, payroll, overtime, reimbursements, performance and KPIs, reports and analytics, access control, and exit management.
  • HR consulting: talent management and compensation strategy.
  • Managed payroll: from hiring to payroll, handling law and compliance.

  • Brands: find creators, manage campaigns, handle content rights, make global payments, whitelist content and use an AI social media manager.

AI, automation and engineering

EKbana

ekbana.com

“Your AI implementation partner.” Its argument is that AI is easy to try and hard to put into production, so AI must be secure, scalable, integrated and value-driven.

  • Approach: build applications with AI at the core, joining business context, data, workflows and people.
  • Roadmap: today, everyday assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini). Year 1, AI-powered business apps for HR, marketing and sales. Years 2–4, your own AI systems on open-source and fine-tuned models. Year 5, AI tailored to each department.
  • Proof: its own HR AI platform cut time-to-hire by 90%.

Crimson Tech

Balkumari, Lalitpur · crimsontech.io

AI-enabled machine vision for zero-defect manufacturing.

  • Products: CT Garud (object detection and segmentation), CT Flow Builder (low-code, no-code), CT OCR (retrainable text recognition) and CT Mikha (pharmaceutical tablet inspection).
  • Applications: child-part assembly checks, defect and anomaly detection, non-contact measurement, high-speed inspection, robot guidance, packaging, welding, batch code and metal OCR, solder, thread and stamping inspection.
  • Industries: automobile, FMCG, electronics and electrical, pharmaceuticals, metal and steel, and precision manufacturing.

Quickfox Consulting

quickrpa.ai

Seen on two business cards only. It offers AI and automation consulting, development, deployment and scale-up.

Gurzu and Emitii

Lalitpur, Nepal and Boston, USA · gurzu.com · emitii.com

Gurzu builds, maintains and scales software with AI-augmented engineering teams.

  • Services: product engineering from MVP to scale, AI integration and automation, team augmentation, QA and test automation, and rescue and maintenance of Ruby on Rails or legacy systems.
  • Numbers: 12+ years, 200+ products shipped, 50+ long-term partnerships, MVP in 3–4 weeks, and a dev team set up in 48 hours. Its industries are SaaS, travel tech, wearables, fintech and e-commerce.
  • Emitii is its newest product, a team workspace with Spaces, Board, Docs, Chat, Timesheets and AI. Teams turn on only what they need, and there is a free start option.

Security, identity and banking

ThirdFactor.ai

A PRIXA company · thirdfactor.ai

“Trust infrastructure built from evidence.” Its modules are identity verification (document checks, OCR, face matching, liveness), AML and compliance screening against sanctions and PEP lists, fraud and risk intelligence, document intelligence, workflow orchestration and trust decisioning.

Nine solutions: customer onboarding, business and KYB verification, account recovery, merchant onboarding, employee verification, government services, insurance claims, agent verification and continuous KYC.

guardsix

guardsix.com

A cybersecurity suite split in two.

  • Detection and network: guardsix SIEM (central log collection, search, investigation, audit reports) and guardsix NDR (network visibility across IT and OT, lateral movement detection).
  • Response and operations: guardsix SOAR (repeatable incident playbooks, automated enrichment and actions) and guardsix Fleet (central management of many SIEMs, built for MSSPs and distributed teams).

Cyber Alert Nepal

Naikap, Kathmandu · cyberalertnepal.com

A cybersecurity organization for businesses, government and individuals, backed by a team with 40+ years of combined experience and recognized by the Indian Embassy for the IIT Madras Pravartak programme.

  • Services: an AI chatbot to report cyber crime to the police Cyber Bureau, asset takedown support for fraudulent or impersonating content, and cyber hygiene reports.
  • Tools and training: 20+ security tools through an SMS suite, Breach Monitor and a gamified Cyber Learning platform, plus workshops, VAPT, IS audit and training.
  • Numbers: 5,500+ users protected, 220+ daily breach alerts and 800+ victims assisted. The core services are free.

Rapid

By Rara Labs · rapidlinks.io

“Nepal’s first comprehensive API banking solution,” installed inside the bank’s own servers so the core banking system is never exposed to the internet.

  • How it works: every request passes through an API gateway, and the system checks the app, its token and the customer’s consent before reading data.
  • Consent model: customers approve each link once through the bank portal with MFA. Tokens are scoped, short-lived and revocable.
  • Services and security: account balance, statements, request to payment, account opening and transaction webhooks. It uses OAuth 2.0, signed JWT, AES-256 and ISO 20022, and partner apps include IMS, Swastik and iRemit.

What the exhibitors have in common

Seven patterns repeat across the brochures.

Shared claims

  • “One platform” pitch: EOS, Trevo, AakashTel, Emitii, Talent Connects and Nimble all promise to replace several tools.
  • Real-time dashboards: EOS, Trevo, Basiyo, AakashSMS and Nimble show live reports as a feature.
  • Cost cuts as the headline: Basiyo 45%, Nimble 40–50%, Nimble’s mission of 50% efficiency.
  • Local-compliance badge: IRD, SSF, Loksewa, connectIPS, Nepal police.

Overlaps between companies

  • HR and payroll: Nimble, Talent Connects and Trevo all sell it, and EKbana uses an internal HR AI.
  • Inventory and POS: EOS, Trevo, Nimble FIMS and Basiyo all cover stock.
  • Messaging and automation: AakashTel, Quickfox and EKbana all sell AI agents.
  • Identity and security: ThirdFactor, guardsix and Cyber Alert Nepal all target trust, from different sides.
  • Workspace tools: Emitii overlaps with Nimble’s project and task module.
  • Software houses: Mantra Ideas and Gurzu both build custom products.
  • Payments: Trevo (connectIPS), Rapid, Nimble (eSewa, Khalti, IPS) and Hamro Patro all depend on local payment rails.

Gaps in the market

Spotted from what the brochures leave out. These are observations, not facts about every company.

Product gaps

  • No education or school management platform, despite Nimble naming schools as clients.
  • No agriculture, supply chain, logistics or fleet software.
  • No Nepali-language AI: AakashTel, EKbana and Veel show nothing on Nepali speech or text.
  • No SME accounting product aimed at small shops; Trevo is a full ERP and EOS covers only restaurants and retail.
  • Few clinical tools: Hamro Health books consultations but no one shows hospital or clinic records.

Business and trust gaps

  • Almost no prices or plan tiers are printed.
  • Case studies are logos and one-line testimonials, not measured results.
  • Different vendors do not show integrations with each other, only with banks and ERPs.
  • Cybersecurity for SMEs is limited to free tools or enterprise SIEM; the middle is empty.
  • Manufacturing AI (Crimson) is not linked to ERP or inventory systems.
  • Many brochures are generic (Mantra Ideas, AMNIL), so little separates them.

Business opportunities

Eleven ideas that combine what the exhibitors sell with the gaps above.

1. SME “starter suite”

A low-price bundle of billing, stock, payroll and SMS reminders sold monthly to small shops, clinics and salons, with IRD billing built in.

Builds on: EOS, Nimble, AakashSMS

2. KYC-as-a-service for small lenders

Package ThirdFactor-style identity checks with Rapid-style consent for cooperatives and microfinance.

Builds on: ThirdFactor, Rapid

3. Managed security for SMEs

A monthly service mixing Cyber Alert’s awareness tools with a lightweight SIEM watched by a shared team.

Builds on: guardsix, Cyber Alert Nepal

4. Smart factory bundle

Machine vision plus inventory and ERP so a defect scan updates stock and quality reports automatically.

Builds on: Crimson Tech, Trevo

5. School and college platform

Fees, attendance, parent SMS and WhatsApp, admissions and staff HR in one Nepal-ready product.

Builds on: Nimble, AakashTel, Mantra Ideas

6. Hospitality and restaurant combo

Join hotel PMS and restaurant POS with loyalty, WhatsApp booking and local payment wallets.

Builds on: Basiyo, EOS-RMS, Hamro Patro

7. AI adoption consulting for mid-size firms

A fixed-price pilot programme that follows the EKbana roadmap but adds RPA quick wins and staff training.

Builds on: EKbana, Quickfox, Talent Connects

8. Emitii for Nepali teams

Localise Emitii with Nepali AI, NPR timesheet billing, eSewa and Khalti payments and templates for schools, NGOs and government offices.

Builds on: Emitii, Gurzu, Nimble
